 The $1,150,000,000 9.625% Non-Step-Up Non-Cumulative Contingent Convertible Perpetual Preferred Tier 1 Securities of $200,000 liquidation preference each (the
“First Tranche Notes”) and the $1,350,000,000 9.625% Non-Step-Up Non-Cumulative Contingent Convertible Perpetual Preferred Tier 1 Securities of $200,000 liquidation preference each (the “Second Tranche Notes” and, together with
the First Tranche Notes, the “Notes”) are being issued by Banco Santander, S.A. (“Banco Santander”) on November 21, 2023 (the “Closing Date”). 

 The First Tranche Notes will accrue non-cumulative cash distributions (“First Tranche Notes
Distributions”) (i) in respect of the period from (and including) the Closing Date to (but excluding) May 21, 2029 (five years and six months after the Closing Date) (the “First Tranche Notes First Reset Date”) at the rate
of 9.625% per annum, and (ii) in respect of each period from (and including) the First Tranche Notes First Reset Date and every fifth anniversary thereof (each, a “First Tranche Notes Reset Date”) to (but excluding) the next
succeeding First Tranche Notes Reset Date (each such period, a “First Tranche Notes Reset Period”), at the rate per annum equal to the aggregate of 5.306% per annum (the “First Tranche Notes Initial Margin”) and the 5-year UST (as defined below) for the relevant First Tranche Notes Reset Period, with such rate per annum converted to a quarterly rate in accordance with market convention. 

 The Second Tranche Notes will accrue non-cumulative cash distributions (“Second Tranche Notes Distributions” and, together with the
First Tranche Notes Distributions, the “Distributions”) (i) in respect of the period from (and including) the Closing Date to (but excluding) November 21, 2033 (ten years after the Closing Date) (the “Second Tranche Notes First
Reset Date”) at the rate of 9.625% per annum, and (ii) in respect of each period from (and including) the Second Tranche Notes First Reset Date and every fifth anniversary thereof (each, a “Second Tranche Notes Reset Date”) to
(but excluding) the next succeeding Second Tranche Notes Reset Date (each such period, a “Second Tranche Notes Reset Period”), at the rate per annum equal to the aggregate of 5.298% per annum (the “Second Tranche Notes Initial
Margin”) and the 5-year UST (as defined below) for the relevant Second Tranche Notes Reset Period, with such rate per annum converted to a quarterly rate in accordance with market convention. 

 Such Distributions will be payable quarterly in arrears on February 21, May 21, August 21 and November 21 of each year (each, a
“Distribution Payment Date”), commencing on February 21, 2024. Banco Santander may elect in its sole and absolute discretion to cancel the payment of any Distribution in whole or in part at any time that it deems necessary or desirable,
and for any reason. No such election to cancel the payment of any Distribution (or part thereof) will constitute an Enforcement Event (as defined below). There are no events of default under the Notes. In addition, under the terms of the Base
Indenture, as amended and supplemented by the First Supplemental Indenture (as defined below), neither the Trigger Conversion (as defined below) nor the exercise of the Bail-in Power by the Relevant Resolution
Authority (both as defined below) or any action in compliance therewith will be an Enforcement Event. 
 The Notes are perpetual. The Notes

November 21, 2028 to (and including) the First Tranche Notes First Reset Date and (ii) any Distribution Payment Date thereafter, and (b) with respect to the Second Tranche Notes on (i) any calendar day during the six-month period commencing on (and
including) May 21, 2033 to (and including) the Second Tranche Notes First Reset Date and (ii) any Distribution Payment Date thereafter, in each case at the
